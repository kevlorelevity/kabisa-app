title: Taking an Uber, Part 5: The Long Ride Home
level: 10
theme: uber
category: transport
culture: The last ride with Kamau — and by now you can banter like a local. Kenyan conversation is seasoned with methali (proverbs) and idioms. "Haraka haraka haina baraka" (hurry hurry has no blessing) when someone's rushing; "Kawia ufike" (delay, but arrive) when you're late; "Umeniwacha kwa mataa" (you left me at the traffic lights) when someone stood you up. Using one at the right moment earns instant respect — and a laugh.
start: Friday night, 10 p.m. You're heading home from a party in Westlands. Kamau picks you up — late, again.
grammar: idioms-proverbs, conditional-nge, continuous-tenses, discourse-particles

@lesson harambee-speech
title: Mgeni Rasmi: Your Harambee Speech
level: 10
theme: community
category: social
culture: "Harambee!" (let's all pull together) is Kenya's national motto and a living tradition: communities raise money together for school fees, medical bills, weddings and funerals. A respected guest — the mgeni rasmi (guest of honour) — gives a short speech, and good speeches are stitched together with methali: "Kidole kimoja hakivunji chawa" (one finger can't crush a louse), "Haba na haba hujaza kibaba" (little by little fills the measure). Today, that guest is you.
start: Your neighbour Mama Njeri is raising school fees for her daughter. The MC invites you, the mgeni rasmi, to speak.

@lesson football-banter
title: Mashemeji Derby: Banter at the Local
level: 10
theme: sports
category: social
culture: Gor Mahia vs AFC Leopards — the "Mashemeji Derby" (the brothers-in-law derby) — is Kenya's biggest football rivalry. The nickname comes from the many marriages between Luo and Luhya families, the clubs' traditional fan bases. Watching at a local bar or kibanda is a festival of trash talk, proverbs and idioms: "Akose achekwe!" (of course he'll score — he'd never live it down), "Maji yakimwagika hayazoleki" (spilled water can't be gathered — what's done is done), "Asiyekubali kushindwa si mshindani" (a sore loser is no real competitor).
