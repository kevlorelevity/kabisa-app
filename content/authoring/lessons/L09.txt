@lesson restaurant-complaint
title: Dinner Gone Wrong
level: 9
theme: food
category: food-drink
culture: Back at a restaurant — but this time things go wrong, and you need to complain clearly without losing your cool. Kenyan complaints are indirect and polite at first, then specific. Continuous tenses carry the story ("tumekuwa tukisubiri kwa saa nzima" — we've been waiting for a whole hour), and "kwamba" reports what was promised ("mliniambia kwamba…" — you told me that…).
start: You and a friend have been waiting at a restaurant in Kilimani for a long time. You call the waiter over.
grammar: continuous-tenses, conjunctions-kwamba, vyo-manner
gl: tumekuwa tukisubiri = "we've been waiting" — perfect of kuwa + -ki- verb #continuous-tenses
gl: saa nzima = a whole hour
gl: Mliniambia = "You (all) told me" #object-infixes
gl: kwamba = "that" — introduces what was said #conjunctions-kwamba
gl: kingekuja = "it would come" — ki/vi agreement for chakula || Kenyans also say "ingekuja" #conditional-nge
gl: jikoni = "in the kitchen" #locative-ni
gl: kulikuwa na = there was
gl: tatizo = problem || Sanifu: ji/ma class (tatizo / matatizo)
gl: Ingawa = "Although" #conjunctions-kwamba
gl: tunaelewa = we understand
gl: hata hivyo = "even so / however" #conjunctions-kwamba
gl: badala ya = "instead of" #conjunctions-kwamba
gl: tuliagiza = we ordered
gl: kuku wa kukaanga = fried chicken
gl: mmeleta = "you (all) have brought"
gl: mbuzi choma = roast goat
gl: kelele = noise
gl: meza ile = that table
gl: wanapiga kelele = "they're making noise"
gl: nyingine = another (n/n)
gl: yenye utulivu = "with quiet" — a quieter one
gl: unavyotaka = "the way you want / as you like" — u- + -na- + -vyo- (how) + -taka #vyo-manner
gl: bili = bill
gl: punguzo = discount
gl: kwa sababu ya = because of #conjunctions-kwamba
gl: usumbufu = inconvenience
gl: Tunathamini = "We appreciate"
gl: uvumilivu = patience
gl: Afadhali = "Better / it's better" #comparatives

  x: Sawa. Na pia, watu wa meza ile walikuwa wakipiga kelele kesho. ; Sawa. Na pia, watu wa meza ile wamekuwa wakila kelele sana.
> Mhudumu: Mnataka kuhamia meza nyingine yenye utulivu? | Would you like to move to another, quieter table?
< Mteja: Ndiyo, tafadhali. Tutakaa popote unavyotaka. | Yes, please. We'll sit wherever you like.
  x: Ndiyo, tafadhali. Tutakaa popote ninavyotaka. ; Hapana, tafadhali. Tutakaa popote unavyotaka.
> Mhudumu: Sawa. Kwa sababu ya usumbufu, mtapata punguzo kwa bili. | Okay. Because of the inconvenience, you'll get a discount on the bill.
< Mteja: Asante. Afadhali kuchelewa kuliko kukosa kabisa! | Thanks. Better late than never!
  x: Asante. Afadhali kukosa kuliko kuchelewa kabisa! ; Pole. Afadhali kuchelewa kuliko kukosa kabisa!

@lesson job-interview
title: The Job Interview
level: 9
theme: work
category: work-admin
culture: Job interviews in Kenya are usually in English, but switching to Swahili for a question or two is a great way to show respect and build rapport — especially with a Kenyan team. Talking about your experience is where continuous tenses shine: "nimekuwa nikifanya kazi…" (I have been working…), "nilipokuwa nikisoma…" (when I was studying…). Purpose clauses use ili + subjunctive: "ili nijifunze" (so that I learn).
start: You're interviewing for a manager role at a Nairobi tech company. The HR lead, Mrs Kamau, starts in Swahili.
grammar: continuous-tenses, conjunctions-kwamba, subjunctive

gl: Bi = Mrs / Ms (short for Bibi)
gl: utuambie = "(please) tell us" #subjunctive
gl: kujihusu = "about yourself"
gl: kuhusu = about
gl: nimekuwa nikifanya kazi = "I've been working" #continuous-tenses
gl: kama meneja = as a manager
gl: kampuni ya teknolojia = a technology company
gl: Nilipokuwa nikisoma = "When I was studying" — -po- + kuwa + -ki- verb #continuous-tenses

gl: nilikuwa nikifanya = "I was doing" #continuous-tenses

< Mgombea: Asante. Kwa miaka mitano, nimekuwa nikifanya kazi kama meneja kwa kampuni ya teknolojia. | Thank you. For five years, I've been working as a manager at a technology company.
  x: Asante. Kwa miaka mitano, nitakuwa nikifanya kazi kama meneja kwa kampuni ya teknolojia. ; Asante. Kwa miaka mitano, nimekuwa nikisoma kazi kama meneja kwa kampuni ya teknolojia.

  x: Nitakapokuwa nikisoma chuo kikuu, nilikuwa nikifanya kazi ya muda jioni. ; Nilipokuwa nikisoma chuo kikuu, nimekuwa nikifanya kazi ya muda jioni.

  x: Wakati mmoja, mradi wetu utakuwa ukichelewa sana. ; Wakati mmoja, mradi wetu ulikuwa ukiwahi sana.

  x: Unavyoona, kiongozi mzuri anasikiliza watu wake zaidi kuliko anavyoongea. ; Ninavyoona, kiongozi mzuri anaongea zaidi kuliko anavyosikiliza.
> Bi. Kamau: Jibu zuri sana. Tutakujulisha uamuzi wetu wiki ijayo. | A very good answer. We'll let you know our decision next week.
< Mgombea: Asante sana kwa nafasi hii. Nitasubiri kwa hamu! | Thank you very much for this opportunity. I'll wait eagerly!
  x: Pole sana kwa nafasi hii. Nitasubiri kwa hamu! ; Asante sana kwa nafasi hii. Nilisubiri kwa hamu!

@lesson kinyozi-news
title: Rains, Roads & Politics at the Kinyozi
level: 9
theme: news
category: social
culture: The kinyozi (barber shop) is Kenya's open-air newsroom: weather, prices, football and politics, all while you get a haircut. Reporting what "they say" uses kwamba and reported speech: "wanasema kwamba barabara itajengwa" (they say that the road will be built). Kenyans love to hedge politely about politics with "sijui", "huenda" (maybe) and "tutaona" (we'll see). The long rains (masika) bring floods; the short rains (vuli) come in October to December.
start: Saturday. You're in the chair at your local kinyozi. Baraka, the barber, starts chatting.
grammar: continuous-tenses, conjunctions-kwamba, conditional-nge
gl: kinyozi = barber / barber shop
gl: nyoa = shave / cut (hair)
